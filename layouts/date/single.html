DA note for you, Miss DeeOpen me

Miss Dee,

Somewhere between the bench videos, the wildly different voice notes, and the photos of my extremely predictable lunch, talking to you became a part of the day I look forward to.

I like how your brain works. How you ask for the full picture. How you can make me laugh and then say something that makes me stop and think. The humour is excellent. The thought behind it is what gets me.

You called it effort, not prep. You were right. This is a little of mine.

I'm looking forward to hearing the laugh instead of imagining it. To letting you finish a story without accidentally replying to the wrong thread. To a day we don't need to over-script.

I'm glad we're doing this. Even if my in-person opening line is somehow worse than the first one.

See you tomorrow, Miss Dee.
Your Absolute Menace
